*[Russian Ark](http://www.wellspring.com/russianark/home.html)* is a movie shot in a single, 87-minute take using one steadicam and a cast of thousands. Certainly a miraculous feat compared to previous films, although when compared to theatre performances where they don't have the luxury of edit, retakes and a narrow focus of attention you have to wonder whether this is just a minor transition.
