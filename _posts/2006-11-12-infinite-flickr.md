I came across a Flickr group called [Infinite Flickr](http://www.flickr.com/groups/infinite/pool/) today. Neat idea: you take a picture of you next to your screen, which has the most recent photo in it. Add it to the group and it becomes the most recent pic for others to do the same. The [result](http://www.flickr.com/groups/infinite/pool/show/) is a long line of 'infinite Flickr photos' in succession. Works quite well.
