Google have just released [Version 2](http://www.google.com/gmm/mylocation.html?hl=en_GB) of their Google Maps Mobile, with the most obvious feature being the ability to locate the user even if they don't have GPS - using the current Cell ID.
From the [press release](http://www.google.com/intl/en/press/annc/20071128_maps_mobile_my_location.html) "[GPS technology] is supported on fewer than 15 percent of the mobile phones expected to be sold in 2007"
Go get it now, point your phone to google.com/gmm
