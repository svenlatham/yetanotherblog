**1. Surf sites at random, or have a set list of regular reads?**I have about ten sites I visit daily. They're all in my links page somewhere or other. Some days I just go out with no particular aim and find random sites that I enjoy, then I usually forget about them :-| I really should blog or, or learn to use favourites.  
**2. Do you visit mostly blogs, or news or other sites?**If they're not blogs, they're usually sites about blogging! I also read [BBC News](http://www.bbcnews.com) and [The Register](http://www.theregister.com). I got introduced to [silicon.com](http://www.silicon.com) today as well.
