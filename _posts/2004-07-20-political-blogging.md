Hansard have done [some research](http://www.hansardsociety.org.uk/node/view/189) into blogs for political means. Looks like an interesting document; I will certainly find time to read it this evening. The Guardian's [interpretation](http://politics.guardian.co.uk/egovernment/story/0,12767,1264628,00.html) of the report is that it is rather negative of the use of blogs for politics, describing it as 'frequently damning'. Would be interesting to see a report on the impact of, and interest in American political blogs, particularly in the run up to November's elections.
