Looks like Google have now applied their tilde ~ fuzzy searching to all searches.

Searching for ["mysql database versioning"](http://www.google.co.uk/search?sourceid=mozclient&ie=utf-8&oe=utf-8&q=mysql+database+versioning) (see next post...) gives results with 'databases' and 'version' highlighted as well. Of course they might just be highlighting the alternative words but not actually using them, I'm not entirely sure yet!
