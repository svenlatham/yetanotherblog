Some of my more recent reads...  
[Angus man diagnosed with chronic lateness condition](http://www.eveningtelegraph.co.uk/news/local/angus-man-diagnosed-with-chronic-lateness-condition-1.124428) - man who has been late for everything has been told it is a condition that affects the same part of the brain as ADHD - via @albiondumsday  
[Those whose data is not easily harvestable risk being a 'New Underclass of Big Data'](http://www.fastcoexist.com/3017102/a-new-underclass-the-people-who-big-data-leaves-behind) - the theory being that people who are paid in cash, don't have Internet access and mobile phones will not be represented fairly when Big Data is used to profile populations.  
A small team at Google has created a [basic web server for the Raspberry Pi](http://googledevelopers.blogspot.co.uk/2013/09/coder-simple-way-to-make-web-stuff-on.html), designed to help people learn to code more quickly and easily.  
Finally, ['The best interface is no interface'](http://www.cooper.com/journal/2012/08/the-best-interface-is-no-interface) - this article from last year still resonates quite strongly with how I feel about technology at the moment. Well worth a read.
