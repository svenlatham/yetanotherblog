Some websites are just useful.  
  
Recently I've had a need to watch file resources. I wanted to know why my hard disk was perpetually active (thinking the worst: a virus). Then I remembered a useful utility on a website called Sysinternals.com - it's called [Filemon](http://www.microsoft.com/technet/sysinternals/utilities/filemon.mspx) and lists disk activity in real time (which processes are opening which files; it's quite interesting in a sad way).  
  
[Sysinternals.com](http://www.microsoft.com/technet/sysinternals/default.mspx) has a **load** of other freeware goodies, all for looking after your computer. As of this evening, Sysinternals looks like it's been taken over by Microsoft which makes a lot of sense considering how many of their KB articles reference its very handy tools!  
  
[BartDart.com](http://www.bartdart.com/) appeared on another dig, and hosts a bunch of slightly more obscure tools for various bits.  
  
[AnalogX](http://www.analogx.com/contents/news.htm) is an old favourite: lots of useful applications for fun and work.  
  
Any other useful (preferably free) sites crammed with the authors' work around that I've missed?
