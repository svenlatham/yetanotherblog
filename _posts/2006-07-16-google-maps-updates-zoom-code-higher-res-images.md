Fantastic. [Google Maps](http://maps.google.co.uk/) finally supports the wheel mouse for zoom. They've also tidied up zooming a little - it seems faster and more responsive. When you zoom in, the current map is animated to zoom whilst the new higher-res map is loaded.

From the [API blog](http://googlemapsapi.blogspot.com/2006/07/revision-258-of-api-v2.html), you can see [Madurodam](http://maps.google.com/maps?t=k&hl=en&q=netherlands&ie=UTF8&ll=52.099467,4.297628&spn=0.000773,0.002704&om=1), a miniature model of a Dutch city (one lucky visitor to my flat will be treated to a day out at Scheveningen, including this - I want to see it but I'm not going alone :-). Zoom out from the image and the scale becomes a bit clearer.
