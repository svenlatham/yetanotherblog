[Chalkhill Lives](http://www.chalkhill-lives.co.uk/) is a soap opera based around *Chalkhill*, a clever little pseudonym for [Paulsgrove](http://www.localhistories.org/Paulsgrove.html), which is an area of Portsmouth, which is just up the road from me :-). 'Chalkhill' refers to the chalk quarries on Portsdown hill just north of the city.

The programme is online, and it's not as bad as I thought, although most of the interest comes from watching the video and thinking 'I know that' or 'I drive through there weekly...'
