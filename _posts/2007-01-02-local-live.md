Aaah good, one of my pet hates in [local.live.com](http://local.live.com/) (Microsoft's answer to Google Maps) has now been fixed. The oblique '*birds-eye*' view now supports continuous scrolling -- it used to require the user to manually select photos as they moved around for any great distance.  
  
The Microsoft offering has some pretty up-to-date imagery, and beats Google for quality in some local areas (the map I was looking for was in Southampton)
