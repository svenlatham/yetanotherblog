Today I had the [live storm tracker](http://www.isleofwightweather.co.uk/live_storm_data.htm) loaded up, watching some impressive thunderstorms track across eastern England from somewhere near Hull to Norfolk. They've all died down this evening, but maybe there'll be some more tomorrow.
I actually had the [PNG representation](http://www.isleofwightweather.co.uk/nexstorm.png) up - it doesn't refresh automatically but takes less resources and is almost as informative at a glance.
