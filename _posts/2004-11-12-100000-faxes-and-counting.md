[FaxYourMP](http://www.faxyourmp.com/) reached its 100,000th fax yesterday. For those who don't know about it, FaxYourMP does exactly what it says on the tin. You enter your postcode to find your local MP, then you can type a message to send as a fax. The entire project is run as a non-profit by a small team of volunteers (two of whom I met yesterday). This milestone is a great achievement.
