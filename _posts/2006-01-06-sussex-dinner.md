Went to the [Sussex Geek Dinner](http://sussex.geekdinner.co.uk/) in Uckfield last night. A great evening, in a nice pub, meeting a group of interesting people. Had a good chat with [Simon](http://www.harriyott.com/index.aspx) who organises these events every few months.  
I definitely prefer the smaller dinners - the one in [London](http://www.thehughpage.com/London_Geek_Dinner_With_Robert_Scoble) last month was okay as an experience but there were *too many* people there (well over a hundred). That might be fine for the popular bloggers or the experienced networkers, but it's daunting and too noisy for the newcomers.  
In comparison, the [Ireland](http://www.thehughpage.com/Irish_Geek_Dinner_With_Robert_Scoble) dinner (about 30) was big enough, and the Uckfield dinner was great. Both times I met loads of people; plenty of contacts and had some good chats.  
I'm definitely looking forward to the next one though - great fun (and if this trackback works as I'm hoping it will, Simon will get this - so cheers Simon!)
