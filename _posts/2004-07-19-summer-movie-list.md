Summer's here, and there are quite a few interesting movies on the way. Here's what I'm planning to go see  
[The Stepford Wives](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0327162/). A group of nerdy gentlemen build their perfect wives from electronics parts. Could be fun. Out 30 Jul.  
[I, Robot](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0343818/). I have yet to be disappointed by a Will Smith movie, although I have low standards and did avoid MiB2 on advice from friends.: Out 6 Aug.  
[The Bourne Supremacy](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0372183/). Matt Damon inadvertantly ends up in a load more trouble. Got to love the car chases. Out 13 Aug.  
[The Terminal](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0362227/). Tom Hanks as a foreigner trapped in an airport terminal in the States after his country ceases to exist during a war, and his passport becomes invalid. Could be very good. Out 3 Sep.
