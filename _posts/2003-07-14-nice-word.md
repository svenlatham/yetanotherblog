Something to add to your vocab: hircine:

hircine

\Hir"cine\, Hircinous \Hir"ci\*nous\, a. [L. hircinus, fr. hircus hegoat: cf. F. hircin.]

1. Goatlike; of or pertaining to a goat or the goats.  
2. Of a strong goatish smell.
