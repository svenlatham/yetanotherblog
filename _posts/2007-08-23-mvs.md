MVS is "a command line client whose purpose is to simplify offline editing of Wiki content". I could have done with this last July when I was writing an offline Wikipedia dumper.
Fortunately, it seems the folks at [Wikibooks](http://www.wikibooks.org/) are now producing [PDF versions](http://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Wikibooks:PDF_versions) of many of their books, making my dumper script largely obsolete.
