I always find the most interesting sites when I'm supposed to be busy doing other things.

[Acts of Volition](http://www.actsofvolition.com) is a site that discusses, in extraordinary detail, various aspects of the Semantic Web, [W3C](http://www.w3.org/) standards and various blogging issues. It's a very nicely designed site and extremely well written - just wish it was on [Blogwise](http://www.blogwise.com/) already ;-).

I'll be adding it to my list of sites shortly, alongside [Dave2002](http://dave2002.pitas.com) - an interesting blog written by David M., who regularly updates with posts reflecting his experience with setting up the site.
