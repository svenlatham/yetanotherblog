Google currently support Flash and image ads (as well as the usual text ads) - now they're adding [Video ads](http://adwords.blogspot.com/2006/05/click-to-play-video-ads-for-adwords.html) to their options. It's an interesting addition - doesn't seem overly intrusive (as long as they don't play automatically, which they currently don't).
