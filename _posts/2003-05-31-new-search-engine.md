New search engine doing the rounds - [turbo10.com](http://www.turbo10.com/), which [The Register](http://www.theregister.com/) [rated quite highly](http://www.theregister.com/content/6/30963.html). That said, I've submitted [Blogwise](http://www.blogwise.com/) (I don't think the search throughput will be either bandwidth-breaking nor significant; it's just cool to be listed alongside [BBC News](http://www.bbcnews.com/) as a reputable source :-)
