[Memeflow Goto](http://www.memeflow.com/goto/) is an AJAX-based service that allows you to build your own homepage. It's a little bit cluttered in my view (why see URL, title, X and # on every single item - it's so visually dense) but still promising.

There seems to be an appetite for this homepage stuff, and [Microsoft](http://www.start.com/), [Google](http://www.google.com/ig) and [Yahoo!](http://my.yahoo.com/) already know that. Look forward to plenty more of these kinds of services from eager programmers in the coming months.
