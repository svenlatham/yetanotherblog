Finally figured out a cheap shot solution to getting screengrabs of all 750-odd blogs on [Blogwise](http://www.blogwise.com). It involves MS Word (I don't have VB), a load of SendKeys commands, [Paint Shop Pro](http://www.jasc.com/), and a build of [Mozilla](http://www.mozilla.org/) 1.2 running fullscreen. Very very tacky, but only because it was 4am and I couldn't figure out a nicer way.

I'm sure [Alexa](http://www.alexa.com/) use something similar (but a bit tidier) like Mozilla in Linux and some clever script to grab their pictures but I don't have Linux with a GUI installed anywhere (don't get me wrong, I like Linux a lot - I have it on three of my five computers, I just can't stand the GUI).
