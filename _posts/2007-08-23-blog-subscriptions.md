Hurrah. [Russell Beattie](http://www.russellbeattie.com/blog/) is back to blogging again. His blog has always been a quite reliable source for mobile news. *Subscribed.*  
I also found a few more blogs today that seemed quite interesting. Subscribed to those too.  
However, [Freakonomics blog](http://freakonomics.blogs.nytimes.com/) seems to have switched to condensed feeds since they moved to the New York Times. Since the majority of my reading is done on a phone on the train, I'm *Unsubscribed.* (and there's rather [a lot of discussion](http://freakonomics.blogs.nytimes.com/2007/08/17/feed-interrupted-another-rss-issue/#more-1774) about this on their blog).
