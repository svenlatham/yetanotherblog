One of the interesting things that's naturally been pushed to the back of the headlines is that this announcement comes *very* shortly after Google have announced an agreement with [SONY BMG](http://www.google.com/intl/en/press/pressrel/google_sonybmg.html) and [Warner Music](http://www.google.com/intl/en/press/pressrel/google_wmg.html), and YouTube announced similar agreements with [Universal](http://www.youtube.com/press_room_entry?entry=JrYdNx45e-0), [SONY BMG](http://www.youtube.com/press_room_entry?entry=2cwCau7cKsA) and [CBS](http://www.youtube.com/press_room_entry?entry=iXG7e1g-BWI).

That's a lot of content suddenly available for GooTube. Definitely a grade up from your average Shakira spoof video.
