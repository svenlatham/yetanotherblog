[Flash Earth](http://www.flashearth.com/) is a flash tool that allows you to compare the satellite photos of a number of online mapping sites, including Google, Ask, NASA and Yahoo (Ask do maps now?).  
  
It's very neat, incredibly responsive, but is taking the photos unofficially so it'll be interesting to see for how long all those sources stay online.  
  
Via [*Google Operating System*](http://googlesystem.blogspot.com/2006/11/flash-earth-compare-mapping-sites.html)
