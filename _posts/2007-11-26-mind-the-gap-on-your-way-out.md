Emma Clarke, the voice of the Underground for eight years has been dropped by TfL after a number of parody 'Mind the Gap' announcements were found on [her website](http://www.emmaclarke.com/fun/mind-the-gap/spoof-london-underground-announcements).  
*"Here we are crammed again into a sweaty Tube carriage ... If you're female smile at the bloke next to you and make his day. He's probably not had sex for months."*  
Despite TfL's apparant lack of humour, a spokesperson told the Evening Standard *"London Underground is sorry to have to announce that further contracts for Miss Clarke are experiencing severe delays"*  
[[Reuters](http://uk.reuters.com/article/domesticNews/idUKL2663927720071126)] [via [UK Mobile Report](http://chiswickken.typepad.com/ringtone/2007/11/mind-the-gap-gi.html)]  
**Update:** While the Reuters article picked the spoof messages as the reason for dismissal, BBC News are [claiming](http://news.bbc.co.uk/1/hi/england/london/7113545.stm) that the reason was "*... because she has criticised the Underground system."*  
*LU said it would not be offering her further work but Ms Clarke said she had been "wildly misquoted".*  
*"[Ms Clarke] said she was "disappointed and perplexed" that LU had not contacted her but instead had decided to dismiss her via the media."*
