New design is up, and I quite like it. More blog-like than the last one, and fits the screen better. I've also put breadcrumbs everywhere and installed a new (better?) gallery. The [last one](http://gallery.menalto.com) was fine - huge and powerful - but that was also its downfall. It was a git to fiddle with and templating was a no-no. [This one](http://singapore.sourceforge.net/) is much simpler, templating is a breeze and it's very hackable. I already added [EXIF](http://www.exif.org/) support to it (okay, so Gallery had that already...)  
  
Anyway, thoughts and comments on the new design very welcome.
