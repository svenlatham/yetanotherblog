Last month I posted about an interesting timelapse video of [Panama Canal](https://www.yetanotherblog.com/2006/07/24/time-lapse-of-the-panama-canal/).  
  
Andrew Denny writes to tell me he has also created a timelapse on the canals - this time of himself, a couple of friends and his canalboat, [Granny Buttons](http://www.grannybuttons.com/) touring the Birmingham Canals in 2001. More info about his 24 hour journey [here](http://www.grannybuttons.com/granny_buttons/2006/04/granny_buttons_.html) and the actual video (it's a four-parter) is [here on YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_hVE6Ebc1n4).

Also via Andrew, [this timelapse](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rJXtfFTOJd8) of 30 canal locks on the Tarbigge Flight, from [rdhs100](http://www.youtube.com/profile?user=rdhs100)
