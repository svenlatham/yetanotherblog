**Keeping Good Time**  
The Raspberry PI doesn't have a hardware clock like regular PCs, so we either have to connect it to the Internet (to sync online), attach an external clock or signal receiver, or completely fake it.  
This is the faking option: [architecture++: fake-hwclock for Arch Linux Arm on Raspberry Pi (using systemd)](http://archplusplus.co.uk/post/40202081414/fake-hwclock-for-arch-linux-arm-on-raspberry-pi-using)
