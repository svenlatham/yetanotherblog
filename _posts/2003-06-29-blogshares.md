I've never really gotten the idea of [Blogshares](http://www.blogshares.com/). It's not that I've ignored it, it's just that I haven't really sat down and tried to understand it.

I feel sort of embarrased now. Casually clicking a link to the [Top Blogs](http://www.blogshares.com/top100.php) revealed that [Blogwise](http://www.blogwise.com/) is 13th (I didn't even realise it was [listed](http://www.blogshares.com/blogs.php?blog=http%3A%2F%2Fwww.blogwise.com%2F)). Apparantly any outgoing shares are worth $25,000, and of the 100,000 shares available none are left. The value keeps rising (currently $622.49 a share) and the current valuation is $306,731.87!

Can somebody please give me a very brief idea of how I start 'playing' Blogshares?
